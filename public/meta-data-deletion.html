Disconnect Meta and delete your RingPort data

Business owners can disconnect a connected Facebook Page or Instagram account from RingPort at any time.

Disconnect immediately

  1. Sign in to RingPort.
  2. Open Settings → Channels.
  3. Choose the connected Page and select Disconnect.

Disconnecting removes RingPort’s stored access token and stops new Meta messages and comments from being processed.

Request deletion

Email privacy@ringport.app with:

  • the RingPort account email;
  • the Facebook Page name or Instagram username; and
  • a clear request to delete connected Meta data.

We acknowledge requests within seven days and delete or de-identify eligible connected-account tokens, messages, comments, and related identifiers within 30 days, subject to limited legal, security, billing, and backup retention requirements.
