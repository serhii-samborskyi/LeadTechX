RingPort Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

What RingPort does

RingPort provides AI receptionist, business messaging, lead management, appointment, and customer-support tools to participating businesses. When a business connects Facebook or Instagram, RingPort processes messages and comments only to provide the connected services that business enabled.

Information we process

We may process account and business-profile information, connected Page or professional Instagram account identifiers, access tokens, message and comment content, sender identifiers, conversation history, appointments, call records, and service diagnostics. Payment card information is handled by payment processors and is not stored by RingPort.

How information is used

  • Operate the receptionist, messaging, lead, booking, and owner-notification features.
  • Authenticate connected accounts, deliver replies, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot service.
  • Maintain legally required records and protect RingPort, its subscribers, and their customers.

RingPort does not sell personal information. Data received from Meta is not used to build advertising profiles or for unrelated purposes.

Sharing and service providers

Information may be shared with the connected business and with hosting, AI, communications, email, payment, security, and support providers only as needed to operate RingPort. We may also disclose information when legally required.

Retention and security

We retain information while a connected business uses RingPort and for limited periods needed for support, security, backups, billing, and legal obligations. Access tokens are encrypted at rest. We use access controls, transport encryption, logging, and operational safeguards appropriate to the service.

Your choices and deletion

A business can disconnect a Facebook Page or Instagram account from RingPort at any time. Account holders and message participants may request access, correction, or deletion by emailing privacy@ringport.app. Include the relevant account email, Page name, or Instagram username. Details are available on our data deletion page.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to privacy@ringport.app.
